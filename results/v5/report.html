    Beliefs

    B1 · works: The constant step function with 50 equal heights has R = 2/3 exactly.
    evidence p0001 · 1 versions
    B2 · conjecture: Peaked non-flat shapes substantially exceed 2/3: free-form optimized bumps reach 0.72-0.84, versus 2/3 for the constant.
    evidence p0003, p0004, p0005 · 2 versions
    B3 · conjecture: A peaked (non-flat) f makes f*f fuller near its maximum relative to ||f*f||_inf, raising R while ||f*f||_1 is fixed by scaling.
    evidence p0003, p0005 · 2 versions
    B4 · conjecture: Symmetry is not a disadvantage: symmetric-constrained optimization matches the asymmetric 0.841166 exactly. Yet the current best (p0019, 0.842478) has its f*f maximum at 0.355 of support, so mild asymmetry appears at the top.
    evidence p0003, p0013 · 4 versions
    B5 · conjecture: Resolution alone gives little (p0019 kept N=100 and still improved); resolution only pays when combined with perturb-restart cycles (p0015 at N=3200, +0.0010).
    evidence p0003, p0005, p0010, p0013, p0019, p0015 · 3 versions
    B6 · conjecture: The optimum is a single dense bump, not a sparse multi-spike comb: p0011's structural moves from p0003 found nothing and p0012's sparse p0005 basin refined only to 0.8278.
    evidence p0003, p0005, p0011, p0012 · 2 versions
    B7 · works: Plain local ascent (random mutation, numerical-gradient Adam, golden-section coordinate ascent) saturates near 0.841; real gains come only from perturb-then-reoptimize restart cycles (p0019 polish +0.0013, p0015 perturb +0.0010).
    evidence p0010, p0011, p0013, p0019, p0017, p0015 · 2 versions
    B8 · mechanism: The hard max in ||f*f||_inf makes the numerical gradient sparse/noisy (only the argmax knot contributes to the denominator), so smooth ascent stalls; replacing max by a soft-max (log-sum-exp, annealed temperature) should give dense gradients and faster ascent.
    evidence p0019, p0017 · 1 versions
