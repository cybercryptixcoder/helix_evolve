## Claims proposed (to discuss)
- Core: LLM discovery systems search where the model's habits lead. Making the space of ideas explicit, a map of
  design decisions, and searching it on purpose finds better solutions with fewer tries, and reaches solutions the
  model wouldn't write by itself.
- Practical: given a problem and its literature, Helix turns the known ideas into a search space and finds the best
  combination, cheaply, on any model.
- Open, later: breakthroughs come from changing what every program assumes; structure substitutes for model size.
