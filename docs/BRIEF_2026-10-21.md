## 2. Notes and decisions

### The seeds question
- A seed (s1, s2, s3) is only the number that seeds Python's random generator for the wide loop's parent sampling and
  inspiration choice (`random.Random(seed * 7919 + gen)` in `helix4/run.py`). Every run, every seed, starts from the
  same Code Zero (a constant function, 0.667). Model sampling isn't seeded at all. Seeds are replicates, not head
  starts. Evidence: control-s1 is the worst run (stuck at 0.8825) while atlas-s1 is the best.

### The claim (see `docs/MILESTONES.md` for the full write-up)
- AlphaEvolve's May 2025 number for this problem: 0.8962 (0.896279944155 exactly), a 50-step function, from DeepMind's
  `alphaevolve_results/mathematical_results.ipynb` section B.2; "a quick experiment" per Georgiev, Gómez-Serrano,
  Tao, Wagner (arXiv 2511.02864), whose later runs reached 0.961 (50,000 steps).
- Same problem and same scoring: our evaluator reproduces DeepMind's own verification code on their construction to
  12 digits (0.896279944155); `tools/verify_ac2.py` checks any construction three ways (Helix, DeepMind's code
  ported, exact rational arithmetic).
- Same set-up shape: AlphaEvolve evolved search heuristics with a time budget (1000 s), warm-started from the previous
  best construction; we do the same with 30 s. Models: Gemini 2.0 Flash + Pro (AlphaEvolve) vs DeepSeek V4.1 Flash
  (ours). Generations/samples for this problem: not published by DeepMind.
- To do when the v4 runs finish: verify every best construction with `tools/verify_ac2.py`.

### Model settings that work (keep them)
- Researcher/planner thinks at `low`; Coders, Mutator, Distiller, Navigator, Compactor at `none` (no thinking).
  From the v3 ablation (low beat none and high) and v4 timing (no-thinking code calls take 3-5 s; a low-thinking code
  call took 6.4 min). Documented in `docs/MODEL_SETTINGS.md`.

### What the v4 data shows (for v4.5)
- The deep loop almost always puts all 3 experiments on the current best program (atlas-s1: 12 of 12 generations;
  most runs ≥ 75%). The population exists but the planner hill-climbs one champion.
- Runs that stall stay stalled: atlas-s3, control-s1, control-s3, journal-s3 sit on the 1/√x construction (≈ 0.8825,
  recalled from the literature) and chase +1e-5 gains. "Gained" counted +1e-9 polishing as success.
- Breakouts came from genuinely different methods (random multi-start; a penalty/continuation method; a resolution
  ladder), noticed by planners that remembered what had failed.
- Programs often stop early (7-13 s of 30) and sometimes return a worse construction than they found.
- A program's printed log carried the key clue once (journal-s2 generation 10).

### Decisions
- v4.5 and v5 live in `helix5/` (reusing v4's sandbox, slots, archive, model client) so the running v4 job's code is
  untouched. Both use the same engine and budgets as v4 (3 deep + 3 wide per generation, 30 s per program, 20
  generations), so tonight's 9 v4 runs remain the baselines.
- Job: v4.5 × 3 seeds + v5 × 3 seeds, side by side, 2 evaluation slots, deadline 7 h; starts after the v4 job ends.
