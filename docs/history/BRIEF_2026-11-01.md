# Brief: November 1, 2026: a pivot to jailbreaking? (Helix Exploit)

## Shreyas's request, condensed
- One project serving two purposes: a paper proposal for the advisor and a GitHub project for internship
  applications (LLM guardrails, LLM security, adversarial AI).
- His worry: LLM-based evolutionary loops are widespread, and breaking one number on one maths problem is not enough
  for a paper.
- His bet: evolutionary jailbreaking is niche enough to leave room for a real contribution.
- His hypothesis: an LLM evolutionary loop (try, see what works, keep going) is suspiciously like probing an agentic
  system for its failure points. A new Helix family, "Helix Exploit", would probe what breaks and what doesn't break
  a target system, keep that in memory instead of algorithmic findings, and use it to break the target. Two uses:
  (1) the loop as the jailbreaker (start here); (2) the loop building or hardening defences.
- Asks: what exists in this space, what a good paper here must show, and whether the idea holds water.

## Landscape found (November 1; details in the conversation)
- Evolutionary / memory-based chat jailbreaking is crowded and moving fast: RedDarwin (arXiv:2610.23726, Oct 2026;
  evolving executable attack skills; strong attacks come from new combinations of reusable mechanisms),
  JailbreakSkill (arXiv:2608.16465; skill library plus failure memory; HarmBench, AdvBench), MemoAttack
  (arXiv:2605.29237; skill-structured attack memory with a lifecycle), ASTRA (arXiv:2511.02356; strategy discovery,
  retrieval and evolution).
- Mapping a model's failure landscape with quality-diversity search exists: Manifold of Failure (arXiv:2602.22291),
  cross-generational MAP-Elites on Gemma (arXiv:2606.00813), ToxSearch-S (arXiv:2601.20981).
- Agents: AutoDojo (arXiv:2606.15057; a six-iteration black-box adaptive loop on AgentDojo against nine defences;
  static evaluation overstates protection). The Attacker Moves Second (arXiv:2510.09023): defences must be judged
  by strong adaptive attacks; 12 defences bypassed at above 90%.
- Recurring weakness across chat-jailbreak papers: success is decided by an LLM judge, and results move with the
  judge.
