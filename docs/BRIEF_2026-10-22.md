## 2. Notes and decisions

### Version naming
Two streams, each with its own lineage:
- **The Atlas stream (conservative):** v4 → v4.5 → **v4.6**. Strict improvements to the v4 architecture: the same
  two loops, Atlas memory and planner, fixed and strengthened.
- **The agentic stream (ambitious):** v5 → **v6**. New architecture: an agent that controls its own generations.

A minor number means the same stream; a major number means a new architecture.

### The mutator observation (checked against the data, October 22)
`tools/analyse_runs.py`, `results/v4cont/atlas-s1`.

The wide loop samples parents with weight 1/(1+rank)/sqrt(1+children) over all valid programs. Two properties of
that rule matter:
- it has a heavy tail;
- it penalises the most-built-on programs, which are the record line.

Measured:
- Generations 1-20: 25 of 60 wide parents were more than 0.01 below the record.
- Generations 21-55: 12 of 105 were.
- Generations 31-55: 63 of 75 wide children landed within 0.001 of the record, so a halo does form later, but most
  of it is "no change".

So the early mutator was largely decoupled from the deep loop by design (the sampling rule), and later the halo
mostly re-runs the record's pipeline for no gain.
